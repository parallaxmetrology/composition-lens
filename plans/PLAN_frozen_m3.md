# PLAN: a template-fit framework for named compositions (written 20 Nov 2026; nothing here has been run)

Working name: composition-lens. New folder; nothing in composition-benchmark, isr-library or other projects is edited. Rules carried over: named readings, no semantics in the structural engine, originals untouched, everything reported, plans amended in dated notes, second pass before hand-over.

## 0. Aim

Take the named compositions that photographers and artists actually use (thirds, phi grid, centre cross, diagonals, golden triangle, rabatment, golden spiral, one/two/three-point perspective) and build a tool that, for any image, reports how well its structure fits each template, against a null, with an overlay and a plain-language reason. Then say, per template, how far that fit can be trusted: recovery on exact synthetic layouts, identifiability between neighbouring templates, and later agreement with labelled data (PICD, CADB).

Gap (from a limited GitHub search, 20 Nov 2026, rate-limited): the public tools score one or two rules (thirds, leading lines) with a heuristic and no per-template validation; datasets carry labels with no detector scored against them.

## 1. Adjustments to the first sketch

1. **Templates are data, not code.** Each template is a geometry spec (primitives: lines, points, paths) in catalogue/templates.json, with its aliases, its PICD and CADB crosswalk, and a status. Only templates whose geometry is unambiguous in standard sources are implemented in v0. Armature (14-line), reciprocal lines and sacred-geometry overlays are deferred until a primary source pins the geometry.
2. **Position profiles are the core reading.** Instead of one yes/no score per template, the engine computes a profile of line strength against position per orientation (horizontal, vertical, two diagonals) and a mass map for point templates. A template's fit is the profile value at the template's primitive positions, ranked against the profile's own distribution (empirical tail probability, Sidak-corrected for the number of primitives). This gives sub-percent position readings (where the dominant horizon sits) as a by-product.
3. **Synthetic first, labelled data second.** Many templates have no labelled data. All are tested for recovery on images whose layout is exact by construction (as in earlier ground-truth work). Labelled validation (PICD, CADB) is M3 and follows the one-shot rules of composition-benchmark.
4. **An identifiability matrix is a deliverable.** For each pair of templates: can the engine tell them apart at a given noise level? Thirds lines (0.333) and phi lines (0.382) are 4.9% of the frame apart; the matrix says whether and when that is resolvable.
5. **Trained models are optional inputs and labelled as such.** v0 uses no trained model. Later inputs (a saliency model, a vanishing-point network) are named as trained wherever they appear.

## 2. Milestones

- **M1 (data):** catalogue + crosswalk (PICD 24, CADB 13, overlay names). Mapping is by name and flagged draft until validated.
- **M2 (engine on synthetic):** evidence extraction, template fits with nulls, exact-layout synthetic generator, recovery and identifiability tests. Scope of v0: position templates (thirds, centre, phi, fifths) as horizontal lines, vertical lines and points; the two main diagonals. Deferred to M2b: golden triangle, rabatment, golden spiral, perspective (needs vanishing-point evidence).
- **M3 (labelled validation):** per-template scorecard against PICD categories and CADB classes, with comparators (Facet's thirds heuristic reimplemented from its public description, the ADadras thirds classifier, a DINOv3 linear probe). Design on dev data; fresh data touched once.
- **M4 (front end):** local-first browser or phone tool: ranked fits, overlays, plain-language reasons.

## 3. Definitions fixed now (M2)

Coordinates: x from left, y from top, both as fractions of the frame; pixel geometry is used wherever angles matter (diagonals).

**Evidence.** Grayscale at a long side of 384 px. Directional edge strength = |gradient component across the line's normal|, from Sobel on a Gaussian-blurred (sigma 1 px) image.
- Horizontal profile Ph(y) = mean over x of |dL/dy|, smoothed along y with sigma 0.8% of the height. Vertical profile Pv(x) likewise.
- Diagonal profile: mean of the edge strength normal to the line, sampled along each line of a family parallel to the main diagonal, offset in steps of 0.5% of the short side, for lines whose clipped length is at least 40% of the frame diagonal; smoothed across offsets with sigma 0.8% of the short side.
- Mass map M = |L - Gaussian(L, 15% of the short side)|, smoothed at 2% of the short side; the point score at (px, py) is the share of total M within a disc of radius 7% of the short side.

**Fit.** For a primitive with profile value v: p = (1 + #{null positions with value >= v}) / (1 + N), null positions = a grid over the interior (profiles: 4% to 96%; points: 12% to 88% in both axes). A template with k primitives gets p_T = 1 - (1 - min p)^k; score = -log10 p_T.

**Readings (always reported):** dominant horizontal line position y*, dominant vertical line position x*, their strength (peak over median of the profile), and the mass centroid.

**Templates in v0** (all positions as fractions): thirds_h (y = 1/3, 2/3), thirds_v, thirds_pts (4 points), centre_h (y = 1/2), centre_v, centre_pt, phi_h (y = 0.382, 0.618), phi_v, phi_pts, fifths_h (1/5 to 4/5), fifths_v, diag_main (both corner-to-corner diagonals; baroque and sinister reported separately).

## 4. Tests and what is pre-stated (M2)

Images: 4:3 (512 x 384) and three other aspect ratios (3:2, 1:1, 4:5), smooth backgrounds with low-contrast texture, one structure placed exactly on a template position with jitter up to 0.5% of the frame (a tone split for lines, a dark disc for points, a tone split along the diagonal), plus off-template distractors at least 6% away, plus Gaussian noise at three levels (0, 0.02, 0.05 of the tone range). Null images: the same structure at a uniformly random position not within 4% of any catalogue position; and texture only.

- **R1 position recovery:** error of y*, x* against the placed position (median and 95th percentile, per noise level). Sanity gate: median error under 1% of the frame at zero noise.
- **R2 template recovery:** top-1 accuracy of the true template among all v0 templates, per template and noise level. Reported, no threshold.
- **R3 detection against null:** per template, AUC of its score on exact-layout images against null images. Sanity gate: AUC at least 0.9 at zero noise for line and point templates.
- **R4 identifiability:** a confusion matrix over all v0 templates (exact layouts, three noise levels) and a resolution sweep: a single line placed from 0.30 to 0.40 in 0.005 steps, reporting estimated position error and which template wins; plus the thirds-versus-phi and thirds-versus-centre separation accuracy.
- Anything failing a sanity gate is reported as a defect in the engine, fixed in a dated amendment with the reason, and rerun; nothing is dropped.

## 5. What this can and cannot support

It can support a statement of the form "on exact layouts the engine recovers X of Y templates, positions to Z% of the frame, and cannot separate A from B below noise N". It cannot support any claim about real photographs until M3, and it does not test whether the templates describe how people see composition.

## M2b: extent, golden triangle, rabatment, golden spiral, perspective (written 20 Nov 2026, before any M2b code ran)

Files: lens/m2b.py (engine), lens/synth2.py (generators), tests/run_m2b.py and tests/analyse_m2b.py. The v0 engine and its results are untouched. All M2b tests are on exact synthetic layouts; real photographs remain M3.

**Definitions fixed now**
- **Extent (line vs compact).** Coverage of the dominant line = share of its length over which an edge exists. Horizontal: rows within TOL (1.5% of the height) of y*, per-column maximum of |dL/dy|, coverage = share of columns above tau = 3 x the image median of |dL/dy|. Vertical likewise. Diagonal: coverage along the best offset of each main diagonal, using the edge strength normal to it. Structure **kind** = the line orientation with coverage at or above tau_c, else 'point' if the blob peak (A1 blob response) is at or above tau_b, else 'none'. tau_c and tau_b are chosen on a separate calibration set (different seeds; results/m2b_calibration.json), then frozen for the tests.
- **Rabatment.** Squares on the short side: for a landscape frame, vertical lines at H/W and 1 - H/W of the width; for a portrait frame, horizontal lines at W/H and 1 - W/H of the height; undefined for a square frame. It coincides with thirds on 3:2 and with the fifths end lines on 4:5; geometry ties are reported as ties, not errors.
- **Golden triangle.** A corner-to-corner diagonal plus the perpendiculars dropped onto it from the two other corners (two orientations). Fit = the minimum over the three segments of each segment's robust z against parallel translations of itself (translations beyond 2 TOL, clipped length at least 40% of the original).
- **Golden spiral.** Fibonacci-tiling quarter arcs (clockwise, ten arcs) in a golden rectangle stretched to the frame; eight variants (two mirrors and a transposition). Edge strength normal to the path, maximised across +/- TOL of the short side; null = the same spiral under random shifts (up to 15% of the frame), scalings (0.85 to 1.15) and small rotations (+/- 10 degrees) that exceed the tolerance; template score = the best variant's robust z minus the expected maximum of eight normals.
- **Perspective.** Line segments from LSD (length at least 6% of the frame diagonal); vanishing points by length-weighted RANSAC (600 trials, 2.5 degree tolerance), removing each family's segments before the next; at most four families. A family is a **finite vanishing point** if it lies within [-2, 3] frame widths and heights of the frame, has at least 12% of the total segment length and at least four segments; parallel families (vanishing point at infinity) are counted separately. Reading: n_finite = 1, 2 or 3 (more reported as '3+'). Templates persp_1pt, persp_2pt, persp_3pt score 1 when n_finite equals K and 0 otherwise.

**Tests and sanity gates (pre-stated)**
- **E1 kind recovery:** confusion over hline, vline, diagonal, point, none at default contrast, 0.10 and 0.05. Gate: accuracy at least 0.9 at default contrast. Also the arbitrated all-template top-1 for the v0 templates (candidates restricted to the detected kind), compared with the unarbitrated 0.72 to 0.85.
- **E2 golden triangle:** AUC of its score on golden-triangle images against diagonal-only images and against shifted-triangle and random-stroke nulls. Gate: AUC against the nulls at least 0.9.
- **E3 rabatment:** recovery at 4:3 (lines at 0.25 and 0.75) and the tie report at 3:2 and 4:5.
- **E4 spiral:** AUC against the perturbed-spiral and straight-stroke nulls, eight-way variant accuracy, and the score against shift (0, 1, 2, 4, 8% of the short side) to read the tolerance. Gate: AUC at least 0.9 for unperturbed spirals.
- **E5 perspective:** exact-count accuracy (1, 2, 3 finite vanishing points, synthetic line drawings with 25% distractor lines) and vanishing-point position error as a fraction of the frame, at noise 0 and 0.02 and at line contrast 0.3 and 0.1. Gate: count accuracy at least 0.8 at default.
- A failed gate is a defect, fixed in a dated amendment and rerun; nothing is dropped.

**Not in scope:** real photographs; a symmetry reading; the armature, radial and sacred-geometry overlays.

## M3: scorecard against PICD labels (written 20 Nov 2026, before any real image was read by the lens)

**Data (read-only, from composition-benchmark).** The held-out single-label pool, 3,312 images, 138 per category, split into DevA (split 0, 1,656 images) and DevB (split 1, 1,656 images) by composition-benchmark/results/split.json. DevA is design data: the lens may be inspected and changed against it, with each change a dated amendment. DevB is confirmation: it is read once, after the spec is frozen and hashed. LockboxB (960 images) is reserved for a later one-shot fresh test and is not touched in this milestone. No PICD image is copied into this folder.

**Features.** For every image, `m2b.analyse` (13 position templates as z and rank scores, kind reading with the three coverages and the blob peak, rabatment, the two golden-triangle scores, the best golden-spiral score, the perspective reading) plus these new named readings, fixed now: the location of the blob peak (argmax of the A1 blob response over the 12% to 88% grid) and its distance to the centre and to the nearest thirds power point; the distance of the dominant horizontal line y* to 0.5 and to its nearest third (1/3 or 2/3), and of the dominant vertical line x* likewise. No trained model is used in any of them.

**Comparators.**
1. A Facet-style thirds and centre heuristic, reimplemented from the public description of its composition analyser (edge-based subject box, distance of its centre to the thirds points and to the centre; the placement score is the larger of the thirds score and the centre score). It is a reimplementation, not Facet's code, and is labelled that way.
2. A fitted linear probe on the lens features (multinomial or one-vs-rest logistic regression, standardised on the training half). This is a fitted layer and is named as one wherever it appears.
3. A fitted linear probe on DINOv3 embeddings (a trained model plus a fitted layer), using the benchmark's saved embeddings for the same images.
Fitted methods train on the other half (DevB to test DevA; DevA to test DevB).

**Primary statistic.** One-vs-rest AUC per PICD category (the category against the 23 others), with a 95% percentile bootstrap interval from 1,000 resamples. Training-free lens scores need no training half.

**Crosswalk predictions (fixed now; each is a one-sided prediction that the AUC exceeds 0.5 with the interval above 0.5).** Template score used for each mapped category: P-RoT and S-RoT: thirds_pts; P-Cent and S-Cent: centre_pt; LS-Hori2: centre_h; LS-Hori3: thirds_h; L-Ver2: centre_v; L-Ver3: thirds_v; LS-Dia and P-Dia: the larger of diag_baroque and diag_sinister; S-Per: the total support of the finite vanishing-point families. That is 11 mapped categories; the other 13 have no training-free lens score and are reported only for the fitted methods. The z score of each template is the one used. Results are reported as counts of predictions met, with every AUC shown; no multiplicity correction is applied and the intervals are descriptive.

**Pairwise position tests (the five pairs that no method separated in composition-benchmark; three have a position reading).** Positive class first; the score is the distance of the dominant line or blob to the centre, which should be larger for the thirds category: (a) LS-Hori3 against LS-Hori2 using |y* - 0.5|; (b) L-Ver3 against L-Ver2 using |x* - 0.5|; (c) P-RoT against P-Cent using the blob distance to the centre. Each AUC is shown with its interval beside a fitted DINOv3 probe trained on the two categories only. Reading rules, fixed now: pairwise AUC at least 0.65 with the interval above 0.5 means the category definitions differ in position as written and the earlier instruments were too coarse for them; an interval inside 0.45 to 0.55 means the two categories do not differ in this reading, which is either overlapping labels or a reading that fails on real photographs, and the detection rate (share of images whose dominant line has coverage at or above tau_c, or whose blob peak is at or above tau_b) is reported to say which: below 30% the test is called inconclusive, not null. P-Dia against P-Hori and P-Dia against P-Ver need multi-point arrangement readings the lens does not have and are not tested here.

**Order.** (1) Smoke test on 12 DevA images (timing, no crashes, sane readings); (2) full DevA extraction and scorecard; (3) design changes on DevA only, each a dated amendment with the reason; (4) freeze the spec (hash of lens/, catalogue/, tests/ and this plan, snapshot saved); (5) one DevB run with the frozen spec; (6) results, honest limits and the fallen list written into the log.

**What this can support.** A statement of the form "template X's fit separates category Y from the rest at AUC Z on a held-out half, and does or does not match a fitted DINOv3 probe". It cannot support claims about photographs outside PICD or about the templates' status as how people see composition.

## Amendments
- **A1 (20 Nov 2026), point evidence.** After the first run, line images were being read as point structures (a line passes through the power points). Point evidence changed from the raw disc share to a blob response: the disc share minus the ring mean (first try, little effect), then minus the ring maximum (kept). Reason: a line has equally high share on both sides along it; a compact structure exceeds all its neighbours.
- **A2 (20 Nov 2026), score definition.** The rank-only score saturated (every strong line and every noise bump could reach rank 1), which let a flat profile in the other orientation beat the true line. Added a robust z (best primitive's value against the null positions' median and MAD, minus the expected maximum of k normals). Both scores are now stored and reported: `score` (z, presence) and `rank_score` (position rank, used to choose among templates of the same kind). The first-run files are kept in results/v0_first_run/.
- **A3 (20 Nov 2026), contrast stress.** Noise at 0.05 barely moved any result, so two low-contrast conditions (structure contrast 0.10 and 0.05, noise 0.02) were added; null images share each condition's contrast.
- **A4 (20 Nov 2026), within-kind accuracy (exploratory).** Top-1 over all templates is confounded across kinds: a compact object on a power point also makes the line profiles peak. Top-1 within a kind (horizontal, vertical, point, diagonal) is reported next to it. Added after seeing R2, so labelled exploratory.
